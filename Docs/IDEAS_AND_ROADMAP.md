# BlueCalx Roadmap

Ideas, experiments and longer-term directions for BlueCalx.

---

# Vision

BlueCalx isn't about writing finished songs.

It's about building musical systems that slowly evolve over time.

The goal is to create pieces that reward long listening through subtle change rather than constant novelty.

---

# Guiding principles

- Build systems, not sequences.
- Favour texture over complexity.
- Let parameters perform the music.
- Give every layer one purpose.
- Prefer evolution over variation.
- Leave room for happy accidents.

# Things worth remembering

Interesting music often comes from:

- tiny timing differences
- subtle detuning
- long envelopes
- independent modulation
- restraint

Not from adding more notes.

---

# Success criteria

A BlueCalx sketch is successful if:

- it sounds interesting after ten minutes
- removing a layer makes it noticeably worse
- every layer has a purpose
- it rewards close listening
- it can comfortably exist in the background
- it still surprises me after several minutes
